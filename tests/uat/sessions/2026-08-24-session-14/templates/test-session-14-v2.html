Feature 1: epic-prefill-status-based (PR #241)

What it was specified to do (CHANGELOG 2026-07-04): the Epic-scoped enqueue_scheduled_prefill selects owned Epic games where status <> 'up_to_date', bounding it to the genuinely-uncached ones. A prefill's validate flips a game to up_to_date and drops it from the set; the sweep re-validates so eviction re-triggers it; new purchases at unknown are still picked up. Its stated purpose is to unblock the Epic cutover — ORCH_SCHEDULED_PREFILL_ENABLED=true plus retiring the host EpicPrefill cron.
